Target grade
This is the grade you want to get. {{ lowestGradeLabel }} tasks are for everyone. Each higher grade adds more tasks. This page only counts the tasks you need for your target grade.
Target date and feedback deadline
The target date is the day to aim to submit a task. The feedback deadline is the last day to submit it and still get feedback from your tutor. @if (unit?.allowFlexibleDates) { In this unit you can change your own target dates in the Task Planner. }
Submit early
Your tutor may ask you to fix something and submit again. Try to submit at least one week before the feedback deadline, so you have time to do this.
Task Planner
It shows the tasks for your target grade on a timeline. Turn on "Show tasks beyond target grade" to see the rest. Click a task to see which tasks you must finish before it.
Download calendar
Pick a grade to save the target dates of its tasks as a calendar file. Open the file with a calendar app, like Outlook or Apple Calendar. If your dates change, download it again.
The two charts
Progress Burndown shows how much work you have left. Task Statuses shows how many of your tasks are at each step.
