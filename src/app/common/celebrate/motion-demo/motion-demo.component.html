Motion

Every state on one page, so they can be judged next to each other. How often a person sees an interaction decides how much motion it gets. A student submits a few times a week, so a submission can be marked. A tutor changes a status a hundred times a day, so that one stays silent.

What stays still

Tutor. Hundreds of times a day.

Marking a task, moving to the next student, and anything reached by a keyboard shortcut get no animation at all. At that frequency motion reads as lag, and the tutor is already looking at the next thing before it finishes.
